Lay AI

Your job assistant · sample replies

    What Lay AI does

    • Answers "where is my application?" from your Applications board.
    • Finds roles on company job boards and job APIs it may read.
    • Prepares an application one click away: tailored resume and cover letter, price shown first.
    • Tracks the portal's application id and reminds you once if you forgot to apply.

    What it never does

    • Press Apply for you
    • Search LinkedIn or Naukri itself (it gives you their links)
    • Invent skills or results that aren't in your resume

    Try it

    Ask the status of "AI engineer intern", find new ones, prepare one, then say "I applied". The application appears on your Applications board and the credits leave your Wallet.
